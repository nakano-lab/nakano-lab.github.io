What it does

When the language changes, the conversation keeps moving.

Choose the spoken and target languages, then start. Alongside manual selection, Full Access can identify which of those two languages is being spoken, one utterance at a time.

01

Transcription in 10 languages

Transcription and translation are more stable. Microphone transcription in a manually selected language keeps running in the free version. Your reading position stays put while you browse earlier text.

02

Three translation modes

Choose Low Latency, High Quality, or No Translation, including a transcript-only view. Free translation pauses for one minute after every five minutes of use.

03

Five summary formats

Choose Standard, Meeting Notes, Lecture Notes, Research Presentation, or Guidance & Procedures to recap the conversation when you need it.

04

Save as text

Save transcripts, translations, and generated summaries in a location you choose. Free summaries and saving share the translation timer and pause.

05

One Mac window, two views

Fold away the controls in the same window, with Start, Stop, and language controls still available. Switch always-on-top on or off independently.

06

The layout adapts to the size and orientation of your iPhone or iPad screen, including readable transcript and translation panes on iPhone Duo.

Auto Detect · Full Access

English, then Japanese.
No language switch in between.

Detection is deliberately limited to the transcription and translation languages you select. Short pauses separate utterances, and each one is labeled and transcribed in the detected language. Follow a bilingual conversation without repeatedly changing the input control.

Auto Detect is available with wayaknow Full Access on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Microphone transcription with a manually selected language remains available in the free version.

Terminology · Full Access

Terms that fit your conversation.

Choose fields and custom words in Terminology Settings. Available in all 10 supported languages and included in your existing one-time Full Access purchase.

Seven broad fields

Medicine & Life Sciences; Chemistry & Materials; Physics & Mathematics; Engineering & Industrial Technology; Computing & Communications; Economics, Finance & Accounting; and Law & Administration. Narrow engineering to Mechanical & Manufacturing, Electrical/Electronics & Control, or Architecture & Civil Engineering.

Your own words, by language

Register correct spellings, readings, and common recognition errors for technical terms or product names. The app keeps standard speech recognition and helps with uncertain spellings. You can also check the original recognition result.

Start with one or two relevant fields. Selecting many fields adds similar candidates and may cause incorrect corrections or increase preparation time before listening and memory use. Not every recognition error can be corrected.

How to use terminology settings

In the app

On Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Apple Watch companion

Keep the latest words on your wrist.

Start listening on your paired iPhone and the latest transcript and translation appear on Apple Watch. Two controls at the top switch the view, and the newest text stays in view automatically.

Speech recognition and translation run on iPhone. Apple Watch does not independently record, start, or stop a session, and it does not sync directly with the Mac app.

Free

Try it at no cost

Microphone transcription keeps running. Translation, summaries, and saving pause for one minute after each five-minute period; select Resume Translation to begin the next period.

Full Access

One purchase, no subscription

Remove the wait for translation, summaries, and saving, and unlock automatic language detection, terminology settings and custom words, and System Audio on Mac and supported iPhone/iPad devices. Restore the purchase on your devices with the same Apple Account.

Requirements & privacy

Designed to keep the conversation with you.

Requirements

An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26.4 or later, iOS/iPadOS 26.4 or later, or watchOS 26.4 or later. System Audio requires Mac or iPhone/iPad with iOS/iPadOS 27 or later. Apple Watch requires a paired iPhone. High Quality translation and summaries depend on Apple Intelligence support and model readiness.

Your data

The App has no user accounts, ads, or analytics SDKs. wayaknow does not collect your audio, transcripts, translations, or summaries on developer-operated servers.
