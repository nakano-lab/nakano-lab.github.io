Speak. Understand. Carry it forward.

Turn speech in 10 languages into live text and translate each completed sentence. With Full Access, wayaknow detects either of your two selected languages for each utterance, following language changes during the same conversation. When you need a recap, choose the summary format that fits.

What it does

When the language changes, the conversation keeps moving.

Choose the spoken and target languages, then start. Alongside manual selection, Full Access can identify which of those two languages is being spoken, one utterance at a time.

01

Transcription in 10 languages

The free version keeps microphone transcription running in a manually selected language, with the newest lines automatically kept in view.

02

Sentence-by-sentence translation

Translate as sentences complete, in Low Latency or High Quality. In the free version, it pauses for one minute after each five-minute period.

03

Five focused summary formats

Choose Standard, Meeting Notes, Lecture Notes, Research Presentation, or Guidance & Procedures. Free use shares the translation timer and pause.

04

Save as text

Save transcripts, translations, and generated summaries in a location you choose. Free use shares the translation timer and pause.

05

Always-on-top mini view

Keep transcription, translation, or both visible in a compact window while using another Mac app.

06

Glance on Apple Watch

Switch between the latest transcript and translation on your wrist while your paired iPhone is listening.

07

Full Access: Auto Detect

Identify each utterance as your selected transcription or translation language, on device, including language changes during a conversation.

Full Access: no wait + Mac audio

Remove the pauses for translation, summaries, and saving, and use authorized Mac System Audio as input. Full Access is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Auto Detect · Full Access

English, then Japanese.
No language switch in between.

Detection is deliberately limited to the transcription and translation languages you select. Short pauses separate utterances, and each one is labeled and transcribed in the detected language. Follow a bilingual conversation without repeatedly changing the input control.

Auto Detect is available with wayaknow Full Access on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Microphone transcription with a manually selected language remains available in the free version.

In the app

On Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Apple Watch companion

Keep the latest words on your wrist.

Start listening on your paired iPhone and the latest transcript and translation appear on Apple Watch. Two controls at the top switch the view, and the newest text stays in view automatically.

Speech recognition and translation run on iPhone. Apple Watch does not independently record, start, or stop a session, and it does not sync directly with the Mac app.

Free

Try it at no cost

Microphone transcription keeps running. Translation, summaries, and saving pause for one minute after each five-minute period; select Resume Translation to begin the next period.

Full Access

One purchase, no subscription

Remove the wait for translation, summaries, and saving, and unlock automatic language detection by utterance plus system-audio input on Mac. Restore the purchase on your devices with the same Apple Account.

Requirements & privacy

Designed to keep the conversation with you.

Requirements

An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26.4 or later, iOS/iPadOS 26.4 or later, or watchOS 26.4 or later. Apple Watch requires a paired iPhone. High Quality translation and summaries depend on Apple Intelligence support and model readiness.

Your data

No account, ads, or analytics. wayaknow does not collect your audio, transcripts, translations, or summaries on developer-operated servers.
