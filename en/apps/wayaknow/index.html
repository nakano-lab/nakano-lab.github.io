wayaknow

Speak. Understand. Carry it forward.

macOSiPhoneiPadApple Watch10 languages

Turn speech in 10 languages into live text, translate each completed sentence into a language you choose, and summarize the conversation in a format that fits the moment.

View on the App Store ↗Help & support

What it does

Follow the conversation in a form you can read.

Choose a spoken language and start. You can change the spoken and target languages from 10 choices while transcription is running.

01

Live transcription

Choose from 10 spoken languages and automatically keep the newest lines in view.

02

Sentence-by-sentence translation

Translate as sentences complete. Choose Low Latency for pace or High Quality for more natural phrasing.

03

Summaries that fit the moment

Choose Standard, Meeting Notes, Lecture Notes, Research Presentation, or Guidance & Procedures to organize topics and key points.

04

Mac system audio

On Mac, authorized audio playing on the device can be selected as the input source.

05

Always-on-top mini view

Keep transcription, translation, or both visible in a compact window while using another Mac app.

06

Save with privacy

Save text only where you choose. On supported devices with ready models, core features process on device.

In the app

At home on Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Free

Try it at no cost

Use microphone input for five minutes at a time. After a one-minute wait, select Resume Translation to begin the next five-minute period.

Full Access

One purchase, no subscription

Remove the session limit and unlock system-audio input on Mac. Restore the purchase on your devices with the same Apple Account.

Requirements & privacy

Designed for on-device processing.

Requirements

An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26.4 or later, or iOS/iPadOS 26.4 or later. High Quality translation and summaries depend on Apple Intelligence support and model readiness.

Offline and your data

Once required models are ready on a supported device, core features can work without an internet connection. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics, and wayaknow does not collect your conversation on developer-operated servers.
