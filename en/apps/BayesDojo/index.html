Foundations

Connect the terms
to the foundations.

Use foundation lessons and the distribution reference to understand the ideas behind an experiment: its purpose, examples, and formulas.

Samples and estimation

Populations and samples, the law of large numbers, the central limit theorem, variance with divisors n and n−1, standard deviation, and standard error.

Likelihood and tests

Likelihood, maximum likelihood, Fisher information, and hypothesis testing. Understand what you estimate and what you compare.

Information

Self-information, entropy, mutual information, cross-entropy, and KL divergence.

Ten probability distributions

Bernoulli, binomial, normal, Poisson, exponential, gamma, chi-square, Student’s t, F, and beta distributions, and their relationships.

Requirements & privacy

On iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Requirements

Requires iOS / iPadOS 17 or later, or macOS 14 or later. Switch between Japanese and English in the app. Use portrait, landscape, and Split View on iPad.

Lessons and calculations run on your device. No account or internet connection is needed for learning. There are no in-app purchases.

Advertising and privacy

The iPhone and iPad versions show banner ads at the bottom of the screen. Lessons remain available when ads cannot be loaded. The Mac version has no ads.

Ads and external links require a connection. The privacy policy explains advertising SDK data handling and consent choices.
