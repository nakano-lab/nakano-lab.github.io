Four workspaces

Understand the ECG from four angles.

Choose a workspace from the top tabs on Mac and iPad, or from the feature menu shown at launch on iPhone.

01

ECG Mechanism

Synchronize excitation, repolarization, the cardiac vector, lead-axis projection, and all 12 leads with a rotatable 3D heart. Break down one lead or compare two to see why each waveform takes its shape.

02

Ion & ECG

Adjust major ion currents and extracellular K⁺, ionized Ca²⁺, Na⁺, and H⁺ (pH). Watch the ventricular action potential and ECG while exploring changes from baseline and links to conditions. Cellular calculations are shown separately from whole-heart estimates.

03

ECG & Conditions

Compare Adult Normal with a representative pattern in one lead or all 12 leads. A common time scale and explanations of the ECG features and their mechanisms help you see what changed—and why.

04

Learn & Quiz

Identify a representative model from 12 leads and a rhythm strip. After answering, review the findings and their mechanisms, then explore them in the simulator.

05

Conditions and physiological differences

Explore illustrative bundle block, atrial fibrillation, LVH strain, and regional ST elevation, alongside a normal child around age five, fever-related sinus tachycardia, and a higher-BMI body-habitus reference.

06

Slow playback and stepping

Slow time to 0.05×, pause, scrub, or step by 10 ms. Manage visible structures and heart opacity from one display panel.

07

Mac, iPhone, and iPad

Larger text and controls, with clearer layouts throughout. On iPhone, use the feature menu and back button, and keep the ECG visible while adjusting ions. The interface supports 10 languages, including Japanese and English.
