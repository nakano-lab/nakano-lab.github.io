Four workspaces

Understand the ECG from four angles.

Move between 3D cardiac activity, ion channels, comparison with normal, and quizzes from the main workspace tabs.

01

ECG Mechanism

Synchronize excitation, repolarization, the cardiac vector, lead-axis projection, and all 12 leads with a rotatable 3D heart. Break down one lead or compare two to see why each waveform takes its shape.

02

Ion & ECG

Follow how sodium, calcium, and potassium currents and extracellular electrolytes alter the ventricular action potential, ECG intervals such as QRS and QT, and lead-specific waveform morphology.

03

Compare with Normal

Place Adult Normal beside patterns such as Right Bundle Branch Block on the same lead and timeline. Compare the changed intervals with their underlying activation sequence.

04

Learn & Quiz

Identify a model from its waveform features, then review the key findings and compare them directly in the simulator.

05

Normal, pediatric, and typical patterns

Switch among adult and pediatric normal ECGs and simplified examples of atrial fibrillation, bundle branch block, axis deviation, ventricular hypertrophy, and ST elevation.

06

Slow playback and stepping

Slow time to 0.05×, pause, scrub, or step by 10 ms. Manage visible structures and heart opacity from one display panel.

07

Mac, iPhone, and iPad

Use the wider overview on Mac and iPad, or keep the 3D heart, explanation, and waveform together on iPhone. The interface supports 10 major languages.
