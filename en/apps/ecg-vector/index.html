Six workspaces

From cell currents to ECGs, six ways to explore.

Choose a workspace from the top tabs on Mac and iPad, or from the feature menu shown at launch on iPhone.

01

ECG Mechanism

A clearer 3D cutaway distinguishes atrial activation from ventricular conduction. Compare projections on the lead axes with new 2D vector diagrams and synchronized traces. Switch between one, two, or 12 leads, choose leads beside the 3D view, and resize the 3D and waveform panels.

02

Ion & ECG

Adjust major ion currents and extracellular K⁺, ionized Ca²⁺, Na⁺, and H⁺ (pH). Watch the ventricular action potential and ECG while exploring changes from baseline and links to conditions. Cellular calculations are shown separately from whole-heart estimates.

03

Ventricular Action Potential

Observe synchronized membrane voltage, ion currents, and gates in the Luo–Rudy I model. Pause at the sodium-current peak. The standard model and stimulus adjustment are free; conductance adjustment, suppression experiments, and standard-trace comparison are included in Pro.

04

Cardiac Rhythm

Follow activation timing from the sinus node through the atria, AV node, Purkinje fibers, and ventricle. Adjust sinus cycle, conduction speed, and AV delay. All features, including schematic bradycardia, tachycardia, and AV-block examples, are free.

05

ECG & Conditions

Compare Adult Normal with representative patterns in one or 12 leads and learn their features and mechanisms. Explore bundle block, atrial fibrillation, LVH, and ST changes, plus reference examples for a child around age five, fever-related heart-rate increase, and higher BMI.

06

Learn & Quiz

Identify a representative model from 12 leads and a rhythm strip. Review the findings and their mechanisms after answering, then explore them in the simulator. Wider screens show waveforms, answers, and explanations together.
