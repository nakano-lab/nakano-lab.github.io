Nakano Lab Software / CODO Monitor

Real-time HRV analysis

CODO
Monitor

Monitoring software that calculates and visualizes time- and frequency-domain heart rate variability (HRV) indices from ECG signals in real time, bringing short- and long-term change into one view.

WindowsMacECGHRV
Visit the Nakano Lab site ↗

Main features

See change now. Review it later.

01

Real-time HRV indices

Calculate and visualize multiple time- and frequency-domain indices as signals arrive.

02

Adaptive personalized alerts

Highlight outliers using thresholds based on the accumulated distribution of an individual’s data.

03

Short- and long-term views

Compare immediate change with longer trends to support situational understanding.

04

Artifact exclusion

Mark care and treatment periods and exclude them from personalized threshold calculations.

05

Annotations and notes

Record medication, care, and other events with timestamps for later review.

06

Logs and figure export

Record indices, timestamps, alerts, and annotations, then export data and figures.

Important

CODO Monitor supports software-based analysis and visualization. It does not provide medical advice or replace clinical judgment. Follow your institution’s operating, ethics, and information-governance requirements.
