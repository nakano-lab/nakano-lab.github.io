What it does

Turn Markdown into a focused reading view.

Choose a file and keep its structure while Hashleaf makes it comfortable to read and easy to navigate.

01

Readable Markdown

Display headings, lists, tables, block quotes, links, and code blocks in a layout designed for each device.

02

Math and code

Render inline and display math so technical documents and research notes remain clear and complete.

03

Search and table of contents

Search the displayed document and move directly to a section with a table of contents generated from its headings.

04

Images and linked files

With Hashleaf Pro, select the containing folder to display images used in the document and open linked files from that folder.

05

Quick Look on Mac

Select Markdown in Finder and press the Space bar for a preview, including rendered math.

06

Share and export PDF

Share the original Markdown file. With Hashleaf Pro, export the displayed document as an A4 PDF on Mac.

Requirements & privacy

Your files stay on your device.

Requirements

iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and macOS 14 or later. Hashleaf supports UTF-8, UTF-16, and Shift_JIS text.

Your data

No account, ads, or analytics. Selected Markdown files and images are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer’s servers.
